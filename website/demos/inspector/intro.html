A support desk, 120 tickets

Each ticket is an actor running the ticket chart. Each open ticket invokes an sla chart that sends sla.breached to its ticket when its deadline passes: 15 minutes for urgent, an hour for high, four hours for normal.
ticket.scxml, its states
new └─ ticket.opened → triage triage └─ ticket.triaged → open open invokes sla (deadline by priority) ├─ sla.breached → escalations + 1, log ├─ ticket.resolved → resolved ├─ unassigned ─ agent.assigned → working ├─ working ─ agent.replied → waiting_on_customer └─ waiting_on_customer ├─ customer.replied → working └─ after 2 h: customer.nudge resolved ├─ customer.replied → open └─ after 24 h: ticket.autoclose → closed closed (final)
tinyactors run: the workbench, a simulated clock
